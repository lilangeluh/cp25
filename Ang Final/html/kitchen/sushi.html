FOOL PROOF SUSHI BAKE

A foolproof crowd-pleaser that layers fluffy rice with creamy, spicy seafood, all topped off with avocado and baked until golden—a no-roll, all-thrill dish.

Ingredients

1 pack imitation crab

2 cups sushi rice

2 tbsp rice vinegar

furikake

1/3 cup kewpie mayo

1/4 cup sriracha

1 avocado

1 tbsp sugar

1 tbsp

1 egg

Instructions

1.

Rinse the rice thoroughly. Combine the rice and water in a rice cooker and set it to cook.

2.

Once the rice is cooked, transfer it to a large mixing bowl. Carefully fold rice vinegar, sesame seeds, and sugar into the rice, being careful to keep the rice grains intact.

3.

Grease a pan with sesame oil or cooking spray. Evenly spread the rice mixture into the pan, pressing lightly to flatten. Sprinkle a generous layer of furikake over the rice and set aside.

4.

Remove any packaging from the imitation crab. Chop it up or process it in a food processor.

5.

Mix the chopped crab with mayo. Season this mixture with sriracha to taste.

6.

Crack an egg into the bowl, beat it slightly, then mix thoroughly to combine with the crab mixture.

7.

Evenly spread the crab mixture over the rice layer in the pan. Top with a thin layer of furikake.

8.

In a small bowl, mix together the remaining mayo and sriracha. Drizzle this mixture over the sushi casserole.

9.

Bake in a preheated oven at 350°F for 25 minutes.

10.

Serve hot, garnished with sliced avocados, sesame seeds, and anything else you like.
