ORANGE YOU GLAD IT'S CHOCOLATE?

Delight in these charming madeleines infused with vibrant orange zest and dipped in rich chocolate—a true reason to be glad, indeed.

Ingredients

2 tsp

1/2 tsp salt

1 tbsp honey

1 tsp vanilla

1 1/2 tbsp orange zest

2 tbsp cocoa powder

1/2 cup

baking mold

1 cup flour

3 eggs

2/3 cup sugar

7 tbsp unsalted butter

Instructions

1.

Combine the flour, cocoa powder, baking powder, and salt in a small bowl. Set aside.

2.

In a separate bowl, whisk together the eggs, sugar, honey, orange zest, orange juice, and vanilla extract until the mixture is smooth.

3.

Gradually incorporate the dry ingredients into the wet mixture, mixing just until combined. Add the butter and continue whisking until the batter is smooth and sticky.

4.

Fold the melted chocolate into the batter until it is evenly mixed throughout. If using a pastry bag, transfer the batter to it; otherwise, cover the bowl with cling film and let it sit at room temperature for one hour.

5.

Preheat the oven to 400°F. Prepare the madeleine pan by buttering and dusting it with flour, tapping out any excess. Fill each madeleine well about two-thirds full, using either a spoon or a pastry bag.

6.

Bake the madeleines for 8-10 minutes until the edges have slightly browned and the centers spring back when touched. Remove from the oven, tap the pan to release the madeleines, and allow them to cool on a wire rack for a few minutes. Dip in chocolate and serve.
